🔥 How to Write Powerful AI Prompts

Most people write weak prompts like: "Help me improve" Professional prompts are specific, structured, and goal-focused.

1. Give AI a Role

Example: "You are a productivity coach" instead of just asking random questions.

2. Explain Your Goal

Tell the AI exactly what you want: confidence, discipline, better habits, communication, etc.

3. Ask for Structure

Ask for: step-by-step plans, routines, exercises, examples, tracking systems.

4. Add Your Level

Beginner? Intermediate? Busy schedule? Low motivation? AI answers become more personalized.
